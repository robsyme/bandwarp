Warp model demo — isoflavone plates

The question: bands on these TLC plates drift vertically across the plate ("warping"), so the same compound sits at different heights in different lanes. Which model of the warp assigns each band to the correct compound row — including when a lane is missing a band, or holds only one faint band? Click bands off, click new ones on, and switch models to feel where each one breaks. Everything here uses real detected bands from the three example plates.

Click a dot to remove that band. Click anywhere else on a lane to add a band there. Drag the small handle at the top of a lane to correct the lane's position (handles turn purple once moved); click empty space in the handle strip to add a whole lane. Dots with a dark ring were found by the warp-guided rescue pass — faint bands hunted where the row curves predicted them — and deserve a second look. The fit re-runs on every change.
